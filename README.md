# XGetawaySX

**Travel Through Us.**
*Discover. Escape. Experience.*

XGetawaySX is a proposed South African travel discovery, marketplace and managed-getaway platform connecting travellers with destinations, accommodation providers, tourism businesses and experiences.

## Product direction
- Travel marketplace for discovery, enquiries and approved online bookings.
- Managed getaways with itinerary options and human assistance where available.
- Trips with 12 permanent categories and XGetawaySX-organised group getaways.
- XGetawaySX Business Class: leisure-first social getaways.
- Family Homes & Smart Stays.
- XGetawaySX Concierge and AI-assisted planning.
- Partner Programme and partner dashboards.
- Partner-only Gallery with stories behind partner images.
- Reviews, satisfaction and customer travel profiles.

## Proposed technology
- Frontend: React, TypeScript and Vite.
- Backend: Django and Django REST Framework.
- Database: PostgreSQL.
- Realtime: WebSockets where required.
- AI: model API integration grounded in verified or partner-submitted facts.
- Media: object storage with explicit rights and permission records.

Start with a modular monolith. Validate technical choices as implementation begins.

## Product principles
1. Never invent partner listings, prices, availability, historical facts or booking confirmations.
2. Gallery advertising approval is separate from approval to receive enquiries or online bookings.
3. Protect customer and partner information with role-based access and privacy controls.
4. Require owner and editorial approval before publishing partner stories.
5. Require documented media rights before promotional publication.
6. Keep customer funds, supplier liabilities, commissions, taxes and platform revenue distinct.
7. Label proposed functionality and illustrative commercial terms until validated.
